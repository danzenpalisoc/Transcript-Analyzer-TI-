How the Analyzer Works

The rules used on every tab.

How an Audit Is Saved

  • Paste the transcript. Interaction ID, start time, duration, direction and the agent are read from its header.
  • Enter the SAP ID. Team leader, operations manager, line of business, locale and VTID are filled from the roster.
  • Choose the analysis (Repeats and Transfer, or Sales) and press Process Script. Pick the audit type and line of business when asked.
  • The AI writes the report. You can edit the summary, the bullets and the flags, and change the Issue Resolved answer.
  • The audit gets an Audit Ref (for example NHA-20261013-0001) and can be emailed to the agent, team leader and operations manager.

How Issue Resolved Is Read

  • Resolved — the answer starts with Yes, or says the issue was resolved.
  • Partially resolved — it starts with Partially or says the issue was partially resolved.
  • Unresolved — it starts with No or Not resolved, or says the issue was not resolved.
  • Not recorded — blank, or a sentence that does not start with a verdict. These are left out of the resolution rate.

Targets Used for Scorecards

  • Avg repeat risk — 30% or lower.
  • Resolution rate — 80% or higher, of the audits that have a clear verdict.
  • Critical flags — 0 per audit.
  • Transfer rate — 20% or lower (Repeats audits). Sales attempted — 80% or higher (Sales audits).
  • Badge — On track when no tile is below target, Needs attention when one is, Priority support when two or more are.

Data & Refresh

  • Sources — the audits saved in the Audit_Log and Dashboard_Data sheets.
  • Speed — your browser keeps a copy so the page opens fast (dashboard rows 30 minutes, audit log 15 minutes, roster 2 hours). Refresh fetches the latest.
  • Generate Slides builds a deck of up to 40 agents from the current filters; Export builds a Google Sheet. Both are shared only with you.
  • Filters apply to Overview, Repeats, Sales and Audit Log together.
